    About

    Hi, I'm Ivan Kovalenko.

    I'm a follower of Jesus with a heart to see people encounter His love, grow in His Word, and live out their faith with courage. Through teaching, mentoring, and the podcast, my prayer is that every person who comes across this ministry is drawn a little closer to Him.

    Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or have walked with God for years, you're welcome here. Grab a coffee, press play, and let's open the Scriptures together.

    — Ivan

    What we believe

    Rooted in the Word

    We believe in one God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; that Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose again; that salvation is a gift received by grace through faith; and that the Bible is God's Word, living and true.
