02 — About

Hi, I'm Ivan Kovalenko.

I'm a follower of Jesus with a heart to see people encounter His love, grow in His Word, and live out their faith with courage. Through teaching, mentoring, and the podcast, my prayer is that everyone who comes across this ministry is drawn a little closer to Him.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or have walked with God for years, you're welcome here. Press play, and let's open the Scriptures together.

03 — Ministry

What we do.

Podcast

Episodes unpacking Scripture and real-life questions of faith, wherever you listen.

Teaching

Bible teaching for churches, youth groups, conferences, and small groups — in person or online.

Prayer

Every prayer request is read and prayed over. You don't have to carry it alone.

What we believe

One God — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus died for our sins and rose again. Salvation is a gift of grace, received through faith. The Bible is God's Word, living and true.
