About

Hi, I'm Ivan Kovalenko.

I'm a follower of Jesus with a heart to see people encounter His love, grow in His Word, and live out their faith with courage. Through teaching, mentoring, and the podcast, my prayer is that everyone who comes across this ministry is drawn a little closer to Him.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or have walked with God for years, you're welcome here. Press play, and let's open the Scriptures together.

— Ivan
